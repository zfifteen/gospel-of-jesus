# Chronological Passage Map

This map lists the primary source locations for material in `book-chrono/`.

| Section | Primary sources |
|---------|-----------------|
| 1. Early Galilee | Mark 1:14–2:17 and parallels; John 3:1–21 |
| 2. Authority and early conflict | Mark 2:18–3:35 and parallels (includes fasting/new wine) |
| 3. Major teaching | Matthew 5–7 (Sermon on the Mount) |
| 4. Parables | Mark 4; Matthew 13; Luke 15; Luke 16:1–13; Luke 16:19–31 |
| 5. Encounters and mission | Matthew 8:5–13; Mark 5:21–43; Mark 6:7–13; Matthew 15:21–28; John 6:25–71; John 10:1–18; John 11 selected; Luke 19:1–10; Mark 10:46–52; Matthew 11:2–24; Luke 10:17–20 |
| 6. Rising conflict | Mark 8:14–21; Mark 8:27–33; Mark 9:2–8; Mark 9:14–29; Mark 9:30–32; Mark 10:17–45; Mark 10:32–34; Luke 12:35–48; Luke 13:6–9; Luke 14:7–14; Luke 17:5–19; Luke 18:1–14; Matthew 18:15–20 |
| 7. Final week | Mark 11–15 (selected units up to trial); Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 23:37–39; John 13; John 14–15 core; John 17 |
| 8. Words from the cross | Luke 23:34, 43; John 19:26–27; Mark 15:34; John 19:28, 30; Luke 23:46 |

All residual units from the Issue #49 audit are now mapped to sequential locations under the absolute completeness requirement of Issue #51. Full verse-level detail continues to be refined as needed.

No post-death material is included.
