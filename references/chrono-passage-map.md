This map lists the primary source locations for material in `book-chrono/`.

| Section | Primary sources |
|---------|-----------------|
| 1. Early Galilee | Mark 1:14–2:17 and parallels |
| 2. Authority and early conflict | Mark 2:18–3:35 and parallels |
| 3. Major teaching | Matthew 5–7 (Sermon on the Mount) |
| 4. Parables | Mark 4; Matthew 13; Luke 15 |
| 5. Encounters and mission | Matthew 8:5–13; Mark 5:21–43; Mark 6:7–13; Matthew 15:21–28; Luke 19:1–10; Mark 10:46–52 |
| 6. Rising conflict | Mark 8:31–38; Mark 10:17–45 |
| 7. Final week | Mark 11–15 (selected units up to trial) |
| 8. Words from the cross | Luke 23:34, 43; John 19:26–27; Mark 15:34; John 19:28, 30; Luke 23:46 |

Full verse-level mapping will be expanded as remaining red-letter units are integrated.
