# Exclusions

This document records material that is deliberately omitted from the main book and the reason for each omission.

**Governing rule (Issue #48):**  
Every inventoried lifetime saying of Jesus must be present in full. There are no exceptions for coherence, length, balance, or any other editorial preference.

Primary categories of permanent exclusion:

- Everything narrated as occurring after the death of Jesus
- All letters, opinions, and writings of other people
- Later theological systems, creeds, and interpretive traditions
- Any speculative reconstruction that cannot be located inside the lifetime record
- Non-speech narrative that does not serve as minimal framing for a lifetime saying or action of Jesus

## Specific Boundary Decisions

### Boundary Correction (Issue #30, 2026-08-03)

The chronological boundary includes all sayings and teachings of Jesus while he was still alive, up to and including the moment of death.

**Rule in force:**  
Only material narrated as spoken or performed by Jesus while he was still alive is eligible. Everything that occurs after the moment of death (burial, empty tomb, appearances, commissions, etc.) remains strictly out of scope.

**Consequence:**  
The seven traditional words spoken from the cross are included because they were spoken while Jesus was still alive:

- “Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.” (Luke 23:34)
- “Truly, I say to you, today you will be with me in paradise.” (Luke 23:43)
- “Woman, behold, your son!” / “Behold, your mother!” (John 19:26–27)
- “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” (Matthew 27:46 // Mark 15:34)
- “I thirst.” (John 19:28)
- “It is finished.” (John 19:30)
- “Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!” (Luke 23:46)

These are present in full in the thematic and chronological books and mapped accordingly.

**Still excluded:** Any material that the narrative places after the death of Jesus.

### Absolute Completeness (Issue #48 / #53, 2026-08-04)

All prior “excluded (logged)” rationales that rested on thematic coherence, chapter length, or editorial balance are superseded and removed.

Every inventoried lifetime saying is now present in full across the thematic book (`book/`), the chronological book (`book-chrono/`), and the Spanish translation (`book-es/`).

No lifetime speech unit remains under an exclusion rationale of any kind.

The only remaining exclusions are:

1. Post-death material (narrated after the moment of death)
2. Non-speech narrative that does not serve as minimal framing for a lifetime saying or action
3. Secondary voices, letters, and later interpretive frameworks

### Confirmation of Alignment

With the completion of Issues #49–#53, the exclusions log, the red-letter inventory, the passage maps, and the methodology document are aligned with the absolute completeness rule. Zero lifetime items remain in any status other than `present in full`.
