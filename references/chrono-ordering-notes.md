# Chronological Ordering Notes

This document records the sequence decisions for the chronological volume (`book-chrono/`).

All decisions rest only on the relative placement of material inside the lifetime Gospel narratives. No post-death framework is used.

## Overall periods

1. Early Galilee (Mark 1–3 and parallels; opening proclamation, first calls, early healings and authority claims).
2. Authority and early conflict (Sabbath controversies, Beelzebul, true family).
3. Major teaching blocks (Sermon on the Mount / Plain material placed in the Galilean teaching period).
4. Parables (Mark 4 / Matthew 13 cluster, with later Lukan parables of lost things placed in the traveling period).
5. Encounters and mission (individual approaches, mission of the Twelve, later encounters such as Canaanite woman, Zacchaeus, Bartimaeus).
6. Rising conflict and journey (passion predictions, cost of discipleship, rich young man, request of James and John).
7. Final week (entry, temple, controversies, anointing, last meal, Gethsemane, arrest, trials).
8. Words from the cross (all seven traditional sayings, ending at the moment of death).

## Parallel differences

Where Mark, Matthew, Luke, and John differ in order, the compilation follows a Markan backbone for the Synoptic sequence and inserts Johannine material at points where the narrative setting is compatible with the lifetime record (e.g., early encounters, temple teaching). Differences are not resolved by later tradition.

## Completeness status

The current files restore the major units previously present in the thematic book and the words from the cross. Extensive Johannine discourses previously logged as excluded for thematic coherence (Nicodemus, Bread of Life, Good Shepherd, Farewell Discourse, High Priestly Prayer, and related units) remain to be integrated in full in subsequent commits to this branch. Their placement will follow the narrative settings supplied by the Fourth Gospel itself.

This document will be updated as remaining units are added.
