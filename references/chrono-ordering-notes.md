# Chronological Ordering Notes

This document records the sequence decisions for the chronological volume (`book-chrono/`).

All decisions rest only on the relative placement of material inside the lifetime Gospel narratives. No post-death framework is used.

## Overall periods

1. Early Galilee (Mark 1–3 and parallels; opening proclamation, first calls, early healings and authority claims; Nicodemus dialogue placed early per its narrative setting).
2. Authority and early conflict (Sabbath controversies, Beelzebul, true family).
3. Major teaching blocks (Sermon on the Mount / Plain material placed in the Galilean teaching period).
4. Parables (Mark 4 / Matthew 13 cluster, with later Lukan parables of lost things placed in the traveling period).
5. Encounters and mission (individual approaches, mission of the Twelve, Bread of Life discourse after the feeding, Good Shepherd, Lazarus, Canaanite woman, Zacchaeus, Bartimaeus).
6. Rising conflict and journey (passion predictions, cost of discipleship, rich young man, request of James and John).
7. Final week (entry, temple, controversies, anointing, last meal with foot-washing teaching and new commandment, Farewell Discourse core units, High Priestly Prayer, Gethsemane, arrest, trials).
8. Words from the cross (all seven traditional sayings, ending at the moment of death).

## Parallel differences

Where Mark, Matthew, Luke, and John differ in order, the compilation follows a Markan backbone for the Synoptic sequence and inserts Johannine material at points where the narrative setting is compatible with the lifetime record. Differences are not resolved by later tradition.
