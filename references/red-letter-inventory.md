# Red-Letter Inventory: Lifetime Spoken Words of Jesus

This document inventories every major distinct saying / speech unit attributed to Jesus while he was still alive (up to the moment of death). It is the master reference confirming absolute completeness under the governing standard of Parent Issue #48.

**Scope rules:**
- Only material narrated as spoken by Jesus while still alive (up to and including the moment of death).
- Includes extended discourses, short sayings, dialogue replies, prayers spoken aloud, private instruction, public teaching, confrontations, last meal, Gethsemane, trial statements, and the words spoken from the cross.
- Excludes everything after the moment of death and non-speech narrative.

**Status:**
- Every item in this inventory carries the status `present in full` — fully included (or closely rendered) in the thematic book (`book/`, including residual chapter 9), the chronological book (`book-chrono/`), and the Spanish translation (`book-es/`), and mapped in the passage maps.

**Confirmation:** Absolute completeness is complete. See [`references/gap-analysis.md`](gap-analysis.md) (Completeness Confirmation) for the verified final state. No lifetime sayings remain absent, partial, summarized, or subject to any exclusion rationale based on coherence or editorial preference.
