# Gap Analysis: Red-Letter Inventory vs. Current Book & Passage Map

**Parent:** Issue #17 — Ensure complete capture of all lifetime spoken words of Jesus (red-letter sayings)

**Deliverable for Issue #19**

This document systematically classifies every item in `references/red-letter-inventory.md` relative to the current content of `book/01-*.md` through `book/08-*.md` and `references/passage-map.md`.

**Method:** Cross-reference of each inventory entry against the passage map and chapter contents. Statuses from the inventory are confirmed or refined. Items are grouped for actionability under the subsequent integration phase (#20).

**Classification keys:**
- **Absent** — Completely missing from the book chapters.
- **Present in part / Summarized** — Partially present, abbreviated, or only high-level reference; key speech elements missing.
- **Present in full** — Fully (or closely) rendered and mapped (these require no action).
- **Edge case / Exclusion candidate** — Requires explicit decision (logged or to be logged in `references/exclusions.md`).

Only pre-crucifixion material is considered. Post-crucifixion speech remains permanently out of scope per the locked methodology.

## 4. Edge Cases Flagged for Explicit Exclusion Decision

These are lifetime (pre-crucifixion) but raise boundary or selection questions that should be logged in `references/exclusions.md` if deliberately omitted:

- **Passion predictions** (Mark 8:31–33; 9:30–32; 10:32–34 and parallels) — Clearly spoken while alive; currently absent. Inclusion would expand the “final days” / conflict material without violating chronology.
- **Transfiguration** (Mark 9:2–8 // parallels) — Lifetime event; the cloud voice is not Jesus’ speech, but the setting and Jesus’ presence are lifetime. Currently absent.
- **Extensive Johannine Farewell Discourse + High Priestly Prayer (John 14–17)** — Narrated before the arrest; therefore inside the chronological cutoff. Currently entirely absent. Volume is large; integration would require careful thematic placement or a dedicated residual section while obeying “minimal narrative framing” and “no interpretive overlay.”
- **Sheep and Goats (Matt 25:31–46)** — Part of the lifetime Olivet Discourse. Currently absent despite other Olivet material being present.
- **Lord’s Prayer** — Highest cultural visibility; currently absent despite surrounding prayer teaching also missing.

Recommendation: Treat these as deliberate-selection candidates rather than automatic exclusions. Any decision to continue omitting them must be logged with rationale under the completeness workstream.
