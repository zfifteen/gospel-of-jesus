# 2. Authority

This chapter gathers statements and actions in which Jesus exercises or claims unique authority while alive: authority in teaching, authority to forgive sins, lordship over the Sabbath, power over unclean spirits and disease, decisive interpretation of the Law, and his unique relation to the Father.

## Teaching with Authority

When Jesus entered the synagogue in Capernaum and taught, the people were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as one who had authority, and not as the scribes.

## Authority to Forgive Sins

When a paralyzed man was brought to him, Jesus said, “Son, your sins are forgiven.” Some of the scribes questioned in their hearts, “Who can forgive sins but God alone?” Jesus, perceiving their thoughts, said, “Which is easier, to say to the paralytic, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Rise, take up your bed and walk’? But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins”—he said to the paralytic—“I say to you, rise, pick up your bed, and go home.” And he rose and went out before them all.

## Lord of the Sabbath

When his disciples were criticized for plucking heads of grain on the Sabbath, Jesus replied, “The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath. So the Son of Man is lord even of the Sabbath.”

On another occasion, when he healed a man with a withered hand on the Sabbath, he asked, “Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do harm, to save life or to kill?” After looking around at them with anger, grieved at their hardness of heart, he said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” He stretched it out, and his hand was restored.

## Authority over Unclean Spirits and Disease

In the synagogue a man with an unclean spirit cried out. Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be silent, and come out of him!” The unclean spirit convulsed him and came out. The people were all amazed and said, “What is this? A new teaching with authority! He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him.”

Throughout the towns and villages he healed every disease and every affliction, and many who were oppressed by unclean spirits were delivered.

## Unique Relation to the Father

Jesus declared, “All things have been handed over to me by my Father, and no one knows the Son except the Father, and no one knows the Father except the Son and anyone to whom the Son chooses to reveal him.”

## Decisive Interpretation of the Law

In his teaching on the mountain he repeatedly set his own word against earlier hearing of the Law: “You have heard that it was said to those of old... But I say to you...” He spoke in this manner concerning anger, lust, divorce, oaths, retaliation, and love of enemies, presenting his own authority as the decisive word.
