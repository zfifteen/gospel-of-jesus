# Preface

This book contains only the teachings and actions of Jesus of Nazareth that are recorded as occurring while he was alive.

No material from after the crucifixion is included.  
No letters, opinions, or writings of other people are included.  
No later theological interpretation is included.

## How to read this volume

The chapters are arranged thematically rather than in strict chronological sequence. Related sayings and actions are gathered so that the core announcement of the kingdom, the exercise of authority, ethical instruction, parables, personal encounters, the demands of discipleship, conflicts, and the final days of public activity can be read as coherent units drawn from the lifetime record.

The chronological boundary is absolute. Only material narrated as occurring while Jesus is still alive (before the crucifixion) is eligible for inclusion. This limit is observed so that the compilation presents solely what Jesus is recorded as saying and doing during his lifetime.

The text consists of Jesus’ own words together with the minimal narrative framing required to make those words and actions intelligible. Nothing is added from later authors, letters, or traditions.

The purpose is to present the message he announced and enacted during his lifetime as a coherent whole, free from subsequent developments.
