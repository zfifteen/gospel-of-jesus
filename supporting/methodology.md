# Methodology

## Governing Rule

Only material that can be located inside the lifetime of Jesus (before the crucifixion) is eligible for inclusion in the main book.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Direct speech attributed to Jesus while alive
- Actions performed by Jesus while alive
- Minimal narrative framing required to make the speech or action intelligible
- Interactions with other people that occur while Jesus is alive

## Exclusion Criteria

- Any scene, saying, or event narrated as occurring after the crucifixion
- All letters, treatises, and writings of other people
- Theological commentary or interpretive overlay from any later source
- Speculative reconstructions that cannot be anchored in the lifetime record

## Editorial Practice

The main book aims for clarity and thematic coherence while remaining strictly inside the chronological and authorial boundary. Organization is thematic rather than strictly sequential, with chronological notes kept in the references directory.
