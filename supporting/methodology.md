# Methodology

This document is the governing rule set for the entire project. All content decisions are measured against it. It is considered locked as of the completion of issue #2, with a boundary correction recorded under issue #30.

---

## 1. Governing Rule

**Only material that can be located inside the lifetime of Jesus (while he was still alive, up to and including the moment of death) is eligible for inclusion in the main book.**

The chronological cutoff is absolute. Anything narrated as occurring after Jesus dies is out of scope, even if it appears in the same Gospel document.

**Boundary correction (Issue #30, 2026-08-03):**  
The previous formulation “before the crucifixion” was overly restrictive relative to the project’s intent. The corrected rule includes the sayings spoken while Jesus was on the cross and still alive. Everything after the moment of death remains excluded.

---

## 2. Inclusion Criteria

Material may be included only if it meets **all** of the following:

1. It is presented in the source narratives as occurring while Jesus is still alive.
2. It consists of one or more of the following:
   - Direct speech attributed to Jesus
   - Actions performed by Jesus
   - Minimal narrative framing required to make the speech or action intelligible
   - Interactions between Jesus and other people that take place while he is alive
3. It can be traced to a specific location in the pre-death portions of the Gospel accounts.

### What “minimal narrative framing” means

Enough context to understand who is speaking, to whom, and under what immediate circumstances.  
It does **not** include theological explanation, authorial commentary on the meaning of the event, or later doctrinal reflection.

---

## 3. Exclusion Criteria

The following are permanently excluded:

- Any scene, saying, appearance, conversation, or commission that the narrative places after the death of Jesus
- All letters, treatises, sermons, or writings produced by other people (including Paul and every other New Testament author)
- Theological commentary, interpretive overlay, or explanatory notes that originate outside the lifetime material itself
- Speculative reconstructions, harmonizations, or elaborations that cannot be anchored directly in the lifetime record
- Later ecclesiastical doctrines, creeds, systematic theologies, or denominational traditions
- Any attempt to explain the significance of Jesus’ death or resurrection (these events, or reflection upon them, lie outside the chronological boundary once death has occurred)

---

## 4. Source Limitation

The only eligible sources are the portions of the four Gospel narratives that describe events and speech occurring while Jesus was still alive.

- Parallel accounts of the same lifetime event may be compared for clarity, but the compiled text must remain faithful to the lifetime setting.
- Differences between the Gospel accounts are not resolved by appeal to later tradition. Where accounts differ, the compilation may present the material thematically or note the variation only if necessary for intelligibility, without choosing a “correct” version on theological grounds.

---

## 5. Editorial Practice

### Organization
The main book is organized thematically rather than in strict chronological sequence. Chronological decisions and notes are kept in `references/chronology.md`.

### Voice
The compiled text presents Jesus’ words and actions. It does not speak in the voice of the project, the compiler, or any later interpreter.

### Quotation and paraphrase
- Prefer direct quotation or close rendering of the lifetime material.
- Paraphrase is permitted only when necessary for clarity or to avoid unnecessary repetition across parallel accounts, and must remain tightly bound to the source.

### Boundary discipline
Whenever a passage raises a question about whether it belongs inside or outside the lifetime boundary, the decision is recorded in `references/exclusions.md` with a brief rationale.

---

## 6. Traceability Requirement

Every item included in the book chapters must appear in `references/passage-map.md` with a clear pointer to its location in the pre-death narrative.

If an item cannot be mapped, it cannot remain in the book.

---

## 7. Change Control

This methodology is locked.  
Future changes to these rules require an explicit decision recorded in the repository (issue or commit) and an update to this document. Silent drift is not permitted.

The boundary correction of Issue #30 is an explicit, recorded change to the chronological rule only; all other rules remain unchanged.

---

**Boundary reminder**  
If a text, idea, or interpretation cannot be shown to belong to the living Jesus (i.e., before or at the moment of death), it does not belong in this project.
