# Editorial Notes

This file records practical editorial practices used in the compilation. It does not alter the locked rules in `supporting/methodology.md`.

---

## Handling of Parallel Accounts

When the same lifetime saying or action appears in more than one Gospel, the following consistent policy is applied:

1. **Primary rendering**  
   A single clear primary rendering is chosen for the main text of the book chapter. The choice prioritizes clarity and readability while remaining tightly bound to the source material.

2. **Recording of variation**  
   Significant parallel wording or meaningful variation among the accounts is recorded in `references/passage-map.md` (and, where relevant, in the red-letter inventory). Parallel references are not repeated inline in every chapter.

3. **No resolution by later tradition**  
   Differences among the pre-crucifixion portions of the four Gospel accounts are left as they stand in the lifetime record. They are not harmonized or resolved by appeal to later tradition, later interpretation, or theological preference.

This practice keeps the book chapters readable while preserving full transparency and traceability in the supporting references.
