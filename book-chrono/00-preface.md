# Preface

This book presents every recorded saying and teaching of Jesus of Nazareth in chronological sequence.

The material is restricted exclusively to the period while Jesus was still alive, up to and including the moment of death.

No material that occurs after death is included.  
No letters, opinions, or writings of other people are included.  
No later theological interpretation is included.

## Relationship to the thematic volume

The original book organizes the same lifetime material by theme. This chronological companion organizes the identical scope by sequence. Both volumes observe the same locked boundaries.

## Completeness rule for this volume

Every distinct saying listed in the red-letter inventory appears here in full. Units previously logged as deliberate omissions for thematic coherence are restored in their narrative place.

## How the sequence is formed

The order follows the relative placement of events and speech in the lifetime portions of the four Gospel accounts. Where the accounts differ in sequence, the variation is noted in the supporting chronology files and one coherent rendering is chosen for the main text without appeal to later tradition.

The text consists of Jesus’ own words together with the minimal narrative framing required for intelligibility. Nothing is added from later authors or systems.

The purpose is to present the full lifetime record of Jesus’ speech in the order the sources themselves support.
